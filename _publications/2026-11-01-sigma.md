**Authors:** Yogesh Rai, Haeyong Chung

**Venue:** IEEE Visualization and Visual Analytics (VIS) 2026, full paper

SIGMA is a declarative JSON grammar for spatial interaction in multi-display visual analytics. A specification defines display entities, spatial predicates, and mapping rules. Predicates such as contact, proximity, alignment, inclination, and pointing are grounded in qualitative spatial reasoning (RCC-8 and DE-9IM) and composed with boolean operators. Rules map satisfied predicates to visualization events and continuous parameter streams that drive D3 and Vega-Lite clients.

We validated the grammar against 96 spatial interaction techniques coded from 34 papers, and implemented a runtime on HoloLens 2 that tracks tablets and phones. Use cases cover the Gapminder, Seattle Weather, and wildfire datasets.
