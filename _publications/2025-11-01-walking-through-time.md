**Authors:** Veronica Vu, Yogesh Rai, Haeyong Chung

**Venue:** IEEE Visualization and Visual Analytics (VIS) 2025, short paper, pp. 351-355

The system pairs an augmented reality headset with a tablet. The headset displays a room-sized 3D timeline; the tablet supports precise sketching, annotation, and sculpting of timeline structures such as curves and branches. A design workshop explored how people used the tool to build expressive timelines for two datasets.

I built the HoloLens 2 authoring application in Unity: spline-smoothed timeline drawing, branching and splitting of non-linear timelines, event cards and annotations, and synchronization with the tablet.
