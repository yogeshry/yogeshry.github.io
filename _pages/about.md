I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the [University of Alabama in Huntsville](https://www.uah.edu) (UAH), advised by [Dr. Haeyong Chung](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=tths4U4AAAAJ). I study **visual analytics**: how interactive visual interfaces help people analyze data and understand AI systems. My research is funded by NASA IMPACT (Marshall Space Flight Center).

Before graduate school, I worked for nearly five years as a software engineer at Cedar Gate Technologies, Centerbase, Datag, and Fetchly Labs, building authentication services, backend APIs, and LLM-based retrieval pipelines.

**I am looking for summer 2027 research internships** in visual analytics, human-computer interaction, and interpretability. [Contact me on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yogeshry).

Research
======

- **Declarative grammars for spatial interaction.** [SIGMA](/publication/2026-sigma) is a declarative grammar for spatial interaction across tablets, phones, desktops, and AR headsets. It composes spatial predicates (contact, proximity, alignment, pointing) into rules that drive visualization events.
- **Immersive analytics.** [Walking Through Time](/publication/2025-walking-through-time) pairs a HoloLens 2 headset with a tablet to author room-scale, non-linear timelines.
- **Visual analytics for mechanistic interpretability.** I study how language models make declarative design decisions when they generate Vega-Lite and Altair visualizations, using mechanistic explanation and feature attribution.
